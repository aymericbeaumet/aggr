## Hello, America

Whatever you need from government, start here.

Describe what you need

Example question: How do I update my address with USPS?

Example 1 of 11: How do I update my address with USPS? An adult and child looking at a tablet among moving boxes

America.gov uses AI to get simple answers only from official government sources. It’s free to use, never includes ads, and keeps your privacy protected.

## 29,000 websites into one.

No more wandering from site to site. America.gov brings the government together in one place.

[See how it works](https://america.gov/how-it-works)

## America.gov keeps your data private.

Your personal information isn’t collected or stored, and your conversation disappears when you leave.

[Learn about privacy](https://america.gov/privacy)

## Clear answers. Official sources.

Every answer is exclusively sourced from federal, state, and local websites.

[Learn about sources](https://america.gov/how-it-works)

## No app to download.

Just open America.gov in your browser on your phone, tablet, or computer.
