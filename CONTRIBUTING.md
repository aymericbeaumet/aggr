# Contributing

For development commands and client ownership, see [client development](docs/client.md)
and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). Run `make check` before submitting a change. Frontend changes
also need `make client-build` and the resulting embedded assets.

## Contribution license

aggr is distributed under the [MIT License](LICENSE), and original contributions are
accepted under those same terms. You keep your copyright.

Include this acknowledgement in your pull request:

> I have the right to submit this contribution and license my original contribution
> under the MIT License.

Obtain your employer's authorization when it owns the contribution. Identify any
third-party code and retain its original notices; the acknowledgement does not
relicense someone else's work. Preserve contributor copyright notices in the source
and distribution. The maintainer should obtain the acknowledgement before merging.

See [licensing](docs/licensing.md) for the project license's scope and third-party
material.
